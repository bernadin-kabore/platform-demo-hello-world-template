## What's already wired up

- **Observability**: OTel traces exported on every request, Prometheus
  metrics at `/metrics`, structured logs to stdout shipped to Elasticsearch.
- **Progressive delivery**: deploys via an Argo Rollout canary, gated by a

- **Service mesh**: Istio sidecar injected automatically, mTLS enforced.
- **Security**: every image is scanned, SBOM'd, and cosign-signed in CI, and
  verified again at admission by Kyverno before it's allowed to run.
